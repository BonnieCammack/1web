I noticed that when your using a float layout, the elements are positioned around text and with Flexbox the elements are arranged within a container.
Flexbox felt easier to use because it made positioning and aligning elements more simple and straightforward. 
The box model helped me control the spacing and size of elements, which made my layout look more organized.
